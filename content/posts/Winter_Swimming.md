---
title: "Winter Swimming"
date: 2026-11-10T08:43:33-07:00
---
Winter swimming in a open air pool with perfect water temps is cool. 

The shock of the cold air getting into the pool and the steam rising from the water in white plumes is like a dream.

Swimming under the water, thinking of the contrast of the steam and clear water under the steam, lends for time to think about what races are up for next year.

Its a nice change from a hot summer. Winter swimming is a welcome change.
